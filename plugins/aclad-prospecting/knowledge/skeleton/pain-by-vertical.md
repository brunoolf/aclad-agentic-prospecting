# Pain by vertical

One section per vertical you sell into. What actually hurts, in the language the
customer uses, not the language of the product.

## Example structure
### Manufacturing
What breaks in their month. What they do today instead. What it costs them.

### Retail
Same three questions.

Industry pain is a hypothesis until supported by account evidence. Retrieve
reviewed methodology from `knowledge/playbooks/manifest.json`; internal-only
source material is excluded from partner-facing generation.
