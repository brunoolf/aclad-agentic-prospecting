# Tone of voice

The partner's reusable profile may offer writing defaults. The seller's private
profile and active scoped preferences personalize them. The current explicit
instruction takes precedence while approvals and commercial exclusions remain.

Ask for a few examples the seller chooses to share; do not scan their whole inbox
to infer style. Record language, formality, preferred openings/closings, words to
use or avoid, and typical length. Examples remain private unless the seller
explicitly chooses eligible personal preferences in an export preview.

Observed edits become candidates. Only an explicit request for a reusable personal
preference activates a rule. A fact about one account never becomes a general
writing instruction. Keep source evidence and support revision/revocation.
