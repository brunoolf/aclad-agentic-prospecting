# Product notes

What you sell, in your own words. This file is why a briefing can say something
specific instead of something generic.

## What it does
The problem it removes, described the way a customer would describe the problem.

## Where it usually comes in
The moment in a company's life when it starts to make sense. Two sites, a second
currency, a headcount threshold, an audit, a system that stopped scaling.

## What it does not do
Being honest here is worth more than any feature list. A rep who knows the
boundary sounds credible; a rep who does not gets caught on the call.

## Modules or components the partner actually sells
Not the full catalog. What this partner implements and supports.
