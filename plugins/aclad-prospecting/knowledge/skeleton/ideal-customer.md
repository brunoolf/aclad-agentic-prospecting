# Ideal customer: interview and report prompts

This is a distributed template, not an active targeting configuration. Private
approved ICPs and campaign bindings live in the runtime Store. Use `build-icp` to
analyze, discuss and review them with the seller; never edit this template as a
second authoritative customer database.

## Three observed reference profiles

- Partner customers: actual accessible customers, with coverage and population.
- Seller customers: actual customers owned by the resolved CRM seller.
- Seller prospects: non-customers worked by the seller; effort does not prove fit.

For each, report geography, industry/subindustry, employees, acquired solutions
and annual revenue grouped by currency/year. Count enterprises once. Show missing
fields, unresolved status, sample/access limits and source dates. Deal value is
not annual revenue. A selected enterprise without a CRM ID remains trackable.

## Strategic targeting conversation

What does the partner sell well? Which territory, company scale and industries
does this seller work? What segment should this campaign test? Explain historical
patterns and personal/partner divergences. A new segment can be a strategic ICP
whose criteria remain explicit hypotheses until supported by evidence.

## Positive signals and exclusions

Prefer observable criteria and reviewed evidence over vague labels like "growing
fast". Preserve commercial restrictions, existing opportunities and contact blocks.
The seller's writing style or preferred target does not remove these restrictions.
Document unresolved free-text exclusions for eligibility review.

## Version and decision

Present criteria, exclusions, evidence/source versions, coverage, gaps, hypotheses
and approver. A proposal is not active. Approval applies to its exact revision;
each campaign stores an immutable approved snapshot. A later ICP does not retarget
old campaigns, even when new results suggest a change. Personal preferences and
shared profile export have separate approval and privacy rules.
