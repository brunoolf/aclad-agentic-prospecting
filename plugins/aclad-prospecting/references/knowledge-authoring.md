`NetSuite Guides/`; those binary files are not included in the plugin package.
The two partner rules paraphrase research/approach methods from Prospecting Quick
Guide pp.39 and 41. They contain no copied cases, prices, statistics or product
claims. Source publication date was not established; null is intentional.

CFO pp.6/10/13–14 supports role-specific priorities during authoring. Manufacturing
personas pp.1–2 is marked internal and remains authoring-only. No universal number
of days for follow-up was established from the inspected sources. Ask the seller
for intervals until a dated, applicable cadence rule is reviewed.

Approval applies to the exact rule, audience and scope, not the entire library.
New sources require filename, page, review date, applicability and allowed audience.
Historical examples cannot substantiate current commercial assertions.
